Writing is a strange journey for me. Sometimes, it feels lonely and dark; at other times, it is spectacular and bursting with life. It takes me through contrasting emotional landscapes, allowing me to encounter different, sometimes contradictory, worlds within myself. And somehow, all these opposing experiences come together to form a harmonious whole—a paradox in which contradiction and harmony coexist.

The loneliness and darkness do not come from having to shut out the outside world in order to concentrate. Rather, they arise when I struggle to grasp the ideas wandering through my mind. These ideas drift like phantoms, flickering in and out of existence. And I become a phantom-catcher, standing alone in the darkness, reaching out to seize them and trap them in bottles. No one can help me, for even I do not know exactly what I am searching for. Only when I finally catch one do I begin to understand what I have been trying to find.

And yet, the journey does not necessarily end there. Sometimes, I struggle to find the words that can give shape to what I have caught: thoughts, ideas, fragments of imagination. It is like standing in an endless sea of flowers, surrounded by blossoms of every hue stretching across the hills as far as the eye can see, searching for the one that resonates with my soul.

Time slips quietly away as I remain suspended between the darkness and this boundless sea of flowers, unsure of where to turn. Until I find the flowers that speak to my heart and bring them into the light for the world to see, this journey remains mine alone—a solitary passage through darkness in pursuit of something I cannot yet name.

If my writing journey were always like this, I might have given up a long time ago.

But then, there are times when everything is completely different. No wandering in the dark. No confusion about which words to use. Thoughts seem to emerge out of nowhere, clear and loud, almost shouting at me: Put me on paper!

And the words come with them, without hesitation—precise, vivid, and alive.

Even when everything around me is quiet, the world inside my mind is bursting with life. Fascinating stories unfold on their own. Living, breathing characters laugh, talk, dance, sing, and sometimes even speak directly to me. Scenes appear one after another, like clips from a series of short films.

I don't have to think things through. I simply record what I see and hear in my mind. I don't have to search for the right words, either. They just leap out of me, as if they have been waiting all along for this very moment.

The most amazing part is that these two completely opposite experiences sometimes come one after another, and sometimes even overlap, drawing me into a world of ice and fire, where I drift between the two extremes—or find myself immersed in both at once.

Sounds crazy, right?

But to me, it’s fascinating. I love it.
