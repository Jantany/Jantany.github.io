Memory

Translated by author from the Chinese original 《记忆》

Sometimes memory is so foolish, so clumsy.

It suddenly picks up forgotten moments
from the dust,
brushes them clean,
then replays them, scene by scene,
like a film.

Yet the things it ought to remember clearly,
it casually tosses away
somewhere they cannot be found.

And so, in some year, some month,
some day, some hour,
I wake on a bright morning
and remember how we met,
came to know each other,
and fell in love.

I remember those days
of leaning on each other,
holding each other close,
staying by each other's side.

And so, beneath the morning sunlight,
I forget
that I no longer love you.

The Deception of Night

Translated by author from the Chinese original 《黑夜的骗局》

Night is the world's most cunning deceiver.

It promises a sky ablaze with stars,
casts no light upon the stone path
beneath your feet.

It seems silent,
serene, remote—
everywhere,
the wind whispers,
the rain murmurs,
the insects sing,
the dogs bark into the dark.

Why, then,
do we still feel our way forward
along such rugged paths?

Why do we sleep so soundly,
drifting into dreams
amid an endless tumult of sound?

Because we dwell within the night.
We are its captives.
We are its constant companions.

Longing

Translated by author from the Chinese original 《思念》

Trying to slip into darkness,
to hide, to vanish, to bury itself deep—
yet blinding light has already engulfed it.

Like dust adrift in the air,
seeking shelter, with nowhere to hide.

On this small expanse of light,
helplessly laid bare in its own smallness,
aching as it soars.

Waiting for the sunlight to fade,
this shameful smallness
hopes, at the very least,
to hold on to a trace
of the warmth
it once knew.
